Available Adventures

Los Dientes del Diablo

This trip is for visitors who enjoy exciting rafting activities. Experience the movement of the river and work together with other members of your group as you enjoy the adventure.

Big Mallard

Big Mallard is a rafting experience for visitors who want to enjoy time on the river with friends and family. It is a good opportunity to enjoy the outdoors and share an adventure with others.

Half Day Adventure

If you have limited time, the Half Day Adventure gives you an opportunity to enjoy a rafting activity without spending the whole day on the river. It is suitable for visitors who want a shorter experience.
